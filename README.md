Match or Hang is a memory and speed game that turns product recognition into a fun, interactive experience. Players match brand products against the clock, and every wrong move brings them closer to getting hanged.

It was built for AI Showbiz at Fortune Institute of International Business (FIIB), using Hindustan Unilever (HUL) as the demo company.

The problem: Consumers see many ads but often forget the products. Traditional promotions don’t encourage active engagement or recall.

The idea: Let people play with the products instead of just looking at them. On an e-commerce platform, winning the game could unlock discounts on the products matched, targeting household budget managers who actively look for deals.

Customisable: Swap in any brand’s products and visuals to use it for a different organisation.
